REFERENCEFilm

Low-loss, stable and controlled. Used as the neutral reference in the listening model.

VINTAGE MODELOil-filled

Models a rounded top end with slightly greater damping and leakage-related softness.

FORWARD MODELCeramic

Models a brighter edge and the nonlinear behavior associated with some high-K ceramic parts.

Important measurement rule

Capacitor material alone does not guarantee a tone. Capacitance tolerance, ESR, leakage, voltage coefficient, microphonics, source/load impedance and circuit position must be matched before attributing a difference to dielectric construction.
